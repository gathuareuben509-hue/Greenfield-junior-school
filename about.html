OUR STORY

Education with Purpose

Greenfield Junior School is a fictional primary school created for a student web design project. Our vision is to provide a supportive environment where children can learn, ask questions and develop their talents.

We value academic achievement, respect, honesty, creativity and cooperation between teachers, learners and families.

Our Vision, Mission and Values

Our Vision

To nurture confident, responsible and lifelong learners.

Our Mission

To encourage learning through quality teaching, creativity and positive guidance.

Our Values

Respect, integrity, teamwork, responsibility and excellence.
